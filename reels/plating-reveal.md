# Reels — Plating Reveal

**Model:** Seedance 2.5  
**Duration:** 6–10 seconds  
**Subject:** the moment a dish is completed

---

## The plating moment

The most powerful moment in gastronomy video is not the finished plate — it's the last gesture that completes it. The final quenelle placed, the last drop of oil added, the herb placed with tweezers. This is the moment that shows the chef's hand and the kitchen's precision simultaneously.

---

## Prompt 01 — Final garnish, tweezers

```
Chef's hand placing single micro-herb with tweezers onto finished plate,
camera at 45° angle, overhead-leaning, watching hand approach plate,
6 seconds duration: hand enters frame holding tweezers, herb placed precisely,
tweezers withdraw slowly — herb remains in exact position,
finished plate visible throughout — Michelin-level composition,
warm 3600K kitchen lighting, photorealistic cooking video,
Alain Ducasse kitchen aesthetic, 4K resolution,
tack sharp on tweezers and herb throughout, medium DOF,
no narration, no title card, continuous single take
```

**The subject is the tweezer placement** — one of the most universally understood signals of fine dining precision.

---

## Prompt 02 — Sauce pour, tableside

```
Dark jus being poured tableside from silver saucière onto white plate,
camera at 35° low angle, watching pour from side,
8 seconds: sauce pours in controlled thin stream,
sauce pools naturally around protein, stops at composition-correct position,
slight steam visible from hot sauce meeting cool plate,
warm 3200K warm dining room light, photorealistic fine dining service video,
Le Bernardin aesthetic, 4K resolution,
tack sharp on pour stream and where sauce meets plate,
shallow DOF — sauce in focus, saucière and background soft
```

**Low angle** for the pour — you see the height of the stream and the moment sauce touches plate.
