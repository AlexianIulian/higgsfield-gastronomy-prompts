# Lighting — Backlit / Transmitted

**Use for:** consommés, broths, translucent ingredients, thin pasta, citrus cross-sections  
**Models tested:** FLUX.2 Pro  
**Key technique:** contre-jour transmitted light

---

## When to use backlit

Backlit photography reveals what top-down photography hides: the depth and translucency of liquid and thin elements. A chicken consommé photographed from above looks like brown water. Photographed contre-jour with transmitted light, it glows amber and the clarity of the clarification becomes visible.

**`amber internal glow visible through glass`** — the payoff of contre-jour on a clarified broth  
**`thin julienned vegetables in suspension`** — the indicator of the skill of clarification (no particulate matter visible)
