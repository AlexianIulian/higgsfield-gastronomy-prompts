# Registers — New Nordic

**Register:** foraged · cold light · overhead · stone · precise anarchy  
**Models tested:** FLUX.2 Pro · soul_2  
**References:** Noma, Geranium, Frantzén, Faviken

---

## The new nordic argument

New Nordic plating rejects the architectural restraint of French cuisine in favour of a different kind of precision — the precision of foraged and fermented elements arranged to look like a cross-section of a forest floor, or a tidal pool, or a compost of beautiful things. The photography must show the intelligence in what appears to be disorder.

**Key visual differences from French haute cuisine:**

| French haute | New Nordic |
|---|---|
| White plate, wide margins | Dark stone, slate, or organic vessel — no margins |
| Protein at centre/offset | Multiple small elements of equal visual weight |
| One sauce, precise geometry | Multiple fermented elements, smears, soil textures |
| Camera 45°, editorial standard | Camera near-overhead, 75–85° |
| Warm 3400–3800K | Cool 5000–5500K or natural window |

## Prompt 01 — Noma register / forest floor

```
Nordic tasting menu course on dark grey slate,
four components: one langoustine tail, small smear of fermented seaweed cream,
two thin slices of raw mushroom, fresh herb sprigs arranged as if grown from the surface,
no geometric arrangement — organic distribution across slate surface,
camera at 80° near-overhead, natural window light, overcast, 5200K,
raking light from left at low angle to reveal slate and element texture,
photorealistic Nordic tasting menu photography, Noma aesthetic,
shot on Phase One IQ4 100MP, 120mm macro, ultra high resolution,
tack sharp on langoustine texture, medium DOF — all elements in focus,
cool grey and green palette throughout, no warm tones,
organic vessel edges visible at frame, no white plate, no plate margin
```

**`no geometric arrangement — organic distribution`** — the most important instruction for Nordic register. The French "clock face" approach is wrong here.  
**`as if grown from the surface`** — the garnish has biological logic, not decorative logic.

## Prompt 02 — Raw ingredient / Faviken register

Before it becomes a dish. The ingredient in its unprocessed form.

```
Three freshly dug root vegetables — parsnip, celeriac, beet — on rough birch bark,
soil still visible on roots, slight moisture on surfaces,
raking outdoor light from left at 10°, natural daylight 5600K,
extreme close-up, tack sharp on soil particles and root surface texture,
negative space on right half — birch bark texture visible,
photorealistic ingredient photography, Faviken/Magnus Nilsson aesthetic,
shot on Leica APO-Macro-Elmarit 100mm, ultra high resolution,
cool natural palette, no colour grading, natural tones only,
no staging, no arrangement — placed as if just brought in from field
```

**The Faviken register:** before cooking. The ingredient is the argument.
